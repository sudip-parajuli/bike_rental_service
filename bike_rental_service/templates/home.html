About Us

Welcome to EasyMoto – Nepal’s Premier Bike Rental Service!

At EasyMoto, we believe in making bike rentals simple, affordable, and hassle-free for every traveler, commuter, and adventure enthusiast in Nepal. Whether you need a ride for your daily commute, a weekend getaway, or an exciting road trip, we provide well-maintained motorbikes at competitive rates.

Why Choose EASYMOTO?

  • Wide Selection of Bikes – From fuel-efficient scooters to high-performance motorcycles, we have a bike for every journey.

  • Seamless Booking – Our user-friendly platform lets you browse, book, and ride in just a few clicks.

  • Flexible Rental Plans – Rent by the hour, day, or week with transparent pricing and no hidden fees.

  • Verified Owners & Secure Payments – We ensure safety with verified bike owners and secure payment options.

  • 24/7 Customer Support – Got questions? We’re here to help anytime, anywhere.
Our Mission

To revolutionize bike rentals in Nepal by offering a convenient, reliable, and user-friendly service that caters to both locals and tourists.
