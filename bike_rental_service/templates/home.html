READY WHEN YOU ARE

Your pickup checklist.

Bring these to the shop
01 / NATIONAL CUSTOMERS

For riders from Nepal

  • National ID, citizenship certificate or passport
  • Valid driving licence for verification
  • Blank cheque or cash deposit
02 / INTERNATIONAL CUSTOMERS

For visiting riders

  • Passport
  • Valid driving licence for verification
  • International Driving Permit (IDP)
  • Security deposit in cash
  • Letter of accommodation or guarantor from Nepal

The deposit is usually NPR 5,000, varying by vehicle and rental duration. Confirm the exact deposit, licence category, insurance, cancellation and refund terms before booking. Ask our team
