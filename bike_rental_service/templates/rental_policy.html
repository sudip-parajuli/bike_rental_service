Privacy information

EasyMoto processes account and contact details, booking information and information you provide for rental verification. The website includes account, booking, payment and chatbot features. Share only the information needed to arrange your rental; do not submit identity documents or payment credentials in the chatbot.

Payment providers process payment details through their own services. Google sign-in, Google reviews, externally hosted bike images and links to WhatsApp or social profiles involve third-party services. Loading an external image or review author photo sends a request to its provider. Google reviews are requested through Google Places when configured and are not saved in our database.

Essential cookies support sign-in and request security. Contact easymotoservices@gmail.com for questions about your information or to request correction or deletion, subject to records needed for existing rentals.

Google services are subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Before confirming your rental

Confirm availability, vehicle condition, your dates, the full price and the required documents with EasyMoto before paying. Daily rates are displayed against each vehicle. We do not offer hourly rentals. The rental day ends at 7 PM; returning after that time adds a NPR 500 night or late-return charge.

The security deposit is usually NPR 5,000, but varies by vehicle, rental duration and arrangement. Confirm the exact amount and refund conditions before booking. We provide a helmet. Fuel is paid for by the renter.

Nepali customers need identity documentation, a valid driving licence for verification and a blank cheque or cash deposit. International customers need a passport, valid driving licence, IDP, cash deposit and a letter of accommodation or guarantor from Nepal.

Confirm cancellation, insurance coverage, damage liability, permitted routes and deposit refund terms directly with our team. These details are not yet published as fixed policies. Inspect the vehicle with our team and agree on pickup and return arrangements.

Maps and reviews supplied by Google are subject to Google Maps additional terms.
